# Third-party dependency - 3D Lighting Studio

## Three.js

- Package: three
- Version used: 0.185.1
- License: MIT
- Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js
- Runtime delivery: jsDelivr CDN

The 3D Lighting Studio uses Three.js for WebGL rendering, camera controls,
geometry, materials, lighting and shadow mapping.
