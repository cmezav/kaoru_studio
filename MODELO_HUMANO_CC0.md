Why one human model is used for three modes:
- Cabeza realista: camera frames the head.
- Busto: camera frames head, shoulders and upper torso.
- Cuerpo completo: camera frames the whole human.
- All three therefore keep identical anatomy and proportions.

The file keeps its morph targets so future phases can expose additional
body/face controls without replacing the base mesh.
