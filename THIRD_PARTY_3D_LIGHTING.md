## Three.js

- Package: three
- Version: 0.185.1
- License: MIT
- Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js

lighting, GLTF loading, Meshopt decoding and shadow mapping.

## Parametric human base

- Local asset: legacy/3d-lighting/assets/models/realistic_human.glb
- Upstream file: public/avatars/parametric-base.glb
- Upstream project: https://github.com/nirholas/three.ws
- Pinned upstream commit: 7a73da13ab987ad016f25dd592682ce95cee7f05
- Original data: MakeHuman / MPFB2 core assets
- Asset license: CC0 1.0 Universal

The upstream project documents that the parametric base is baked from
CC0 MakeHuman/MPFB2 data. It includes a humanoid skeleton and identity
morph targets. No attribution is legally required for the CC0 source
assets, but provenance is kept here for clarity.
